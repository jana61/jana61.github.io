About Jana Walid · Data & Development

I'm a Data Science student at Alexandria University with a simple belief: data should make decisions easier, not harder. That curiosity turned into hands-on experience, building interactive dashboards in Power BI and Excel, and going deeper with R, Python, and Java to uncover what a chart alone can't show, from predicting late deliveries with machine learning to building a role-based system from scratch in Java.


Data analysis has genuinely become a passion of mine. Five dashboards in, from workforce analytics to clinical health data, I still get the same rush from watching a messy spreadsheet turn into a clear, visual story, and I keep looking for the next dataset that needs one.


What sets me apart is that range. I'm just as comfortable designing a clean, client-ready dashboard as I am running clustering and decision-tree models behind it, so you don't just get a good-looking chart, you get the analysis that explains why it looks that way.


I'm detail-oriented, communicate findings in plain language, and take deadlines seriously.

Superstore Sales Dashboard

Power BI · Power Query · DAX · Sales Analytics

What I did: Cleaned the Superstore data in Power Query and built calculated columns and DAX measures, including a monthly running total, year-over-year growth, and average shipping delay. Added product category and order year slicers, a KPI row, a quarterly drill-down table, and a regional sales map.

What you get: A quick read on 5,009 orders, $1.10M in sales and $132.5K in profit, plus how sales and orders trend from 2016 to 2019 and where the revenue comes from.

HR Dashboard HR Analytics

HR Dashboards (2 Projects)

Power BI · Workforce & Attrition Analytics

What I did: Built two Power BI dashboards on employee data. The first covers workforce composition with KPI cards, salary by department, recruitment sources, performance and satisfaction, and a headcount map. The second digs into attrition, job satisfaction, work-life balance, diversity and demographics across HR, R&D and Sales, with filtering by job role and education. Data cleaning was done in Power Query, with custom groups and DAX measures.

What you get: A clear picture of who works in the company (311 employees, 67% active, 33% terminated), what drives turnover, and how satisfied and how well paid each group is.

Food Delivery Analysis

Power BI · R · R Shiny · Team Project (9 members)

What I did: Worked with a team of nine on an R Shiny app covering data cleaning, feature engineering, Apriori association rules, K-Means clustering and a decision tree for late deliveries. I also built the Power BI dashboard shown here, cleaned in Power Query, with DAX categories for distance, delivery speed and preparation time, and slicers for vehicle and time of day.

What you get: An overview of 912 deliveries (average 56.45 min delivery time over 10.07 km) and how weather, traffic, vehicle type and courier experience relate to delivery performance.
